the heart is designed in a manner that the lights light up in the flow of blood, going from the right upper, right lower, left lower and finally left upper chamber of the heart
I made the project this way simply because I liked the idea of the heart; my passion for learning about health and by extension anatomy; I also liked how it served as a tribute to my friend, an expression through design of the project and the words written on it
